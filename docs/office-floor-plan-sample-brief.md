# Office floor plan: a sample brief (R115, the first test)

Start a project from the **Workplace: tech** template, then run the **Office floor plan** playbook from the chat ("plan this office floor") or from the run page, with these notes:

> Northwind Labs, a software firm, is taking one floor: about 1,000 m², roughly 40 m by 25 m, with a central core (two lifts, two stairs, toilets, a shaft).
> 60 people now, room to grow to 70. Mostly open desks in team pods; two cabins for the founders.
> 4 meeting rooms (two small for 4, one for 8, one large for 14), 6 phone booths, 2 focus rooms.
> A reception with a small visitor lounge by the lift lobby. A pantry and a breakout that can double as a town hall.
> A server or hub room, a store, lockers. Natural light for the desks.

What each step should give:

1. **Brief to programme**: Innowa writes the programme (60 seats, the rooms above) and asks only what it has to (for instance, whether the 70 is planned now).
2. **The programme**: you confirm it, or send it back with what to change.
3. **The empty floor**: Innowa draws the 40 m by 25 m plate, the core in the middle and the entrance door, and shows you a picture.
4. **First layouts**: the test fit offers a few furnished layouts.
5. **Pick a layout**: you pick one; it goes into the project as one undoable step.
6. **Tidy against the programme**: Innowa lists what is short, fixes what is clear, and asks you about the choices.
7. **Accept the plan**: you put it in the project, or send it back.
