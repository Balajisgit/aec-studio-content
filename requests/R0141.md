# R141 · Innowa and playbooks keep layouts as design options, asking where each goes

Owner: 'when innowa creates either on its own or theu playbook - design options - it will ask to keep as options instead of only one'. A playbook that offers several layouts (the test fit) lets the owner tick the ones to keep, and asks (owner: 'it should ask') whether the first goes into the design open or every one becomes a new design option under the same programme option (named after its layout, own log and undo); the rest stay in the run's record. Innowa in the chat, about to lay out a floor that already has one, asks: into the design open, or as a new design; several variants it offers to keep as options. A design with nothing drawn: no question. Changes in the app's run engine and Innowa's tools, and in the content's playbooks and skill; the test fit script and the kernel are unchanged.
