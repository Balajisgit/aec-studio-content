# R144 · Relationships between Objects in the Library, applied by the Studio; the kernel only evaluates parameters

Owner: 'instead of hard coding the relationship rules in the kernel, we will supply it thru library thru relationship js or something like that', then 'ok'; 'No object level relationships in the kernel'; 'Walls at defined height, then places a beam on it, a slab over beam'. Relationship rules live in the Library (content): e.g. a wall's height from the storey less the slab and beam above, a beam on a wall's top, a slab over the beams, a stair below cutting the slab. The Studio applies them after each step (beside defaults.js), writing the parameters of auto Objects in the same undoable step; anything set by hand is left alone and flagged under To look at when it no longer fits. The kernel evaluates each Object from its parameters only: its slab-above value (levelScope) and the stair's cut in the slab above are taken out, and the Library's wall Objects take a plain height.
