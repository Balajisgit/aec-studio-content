# R66 · Levels in model 4.0: heights from levels, walls through floors, columns in walls, stair and void holes in slabs

The owner (11 Nov 2026), on what the 4.0 kernel still needs from the old one: "ya make it as requests". What 3.0 had and 4.0 lacks, rewritten for primers, roles and Objects: (1) Heights from levels (old heights.js): an Object's formulas see its level's height (storey) and the slab above, so a wall reaches the floor above by default instead of a fixed 3000; a line may rise to a higher level (top {level, offset}); doors and windows sit from their level's floor; the plan's cut height. (2) Walls through floors (old topology.js passingSegments, levelGraph): a wall rising through a floor shows on that floor's plan and bounds its faces. (3) Columns in walls (old plan.js columnFootprints): a wall stops at the face of a column standing in it (the corner solver treats the column as an obstacle). (4) Holes in slabs (old plan.js stairHoles, levelVoids): a stair cuts its outline out of the slab above; a double-height face makes a hole in the slab over it.
