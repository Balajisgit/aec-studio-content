# R70 · Easy drawing 3/5: the Room tool, with clear (inside) sizes

The owner (11 Nov 2026): "ya make it as requests to go by the order". Third of five. Drag a rectangle, or type its size, to get four walls, the face and Room + Slab in one step; a wall shared with the next room is reused, not doubled. Sizes can be clear (3000 × 3600 inside the walls, for carpet area): the kernel places the centre lines from the walls' thicknesses. Polygon and L-shaped rooms, and the footprint (Fit to plot) to external walls in one click.
