# R114 · The Library as a catalogue, with Objects, Layouts and Templates

Discussed in session 41. The Library is organized as a catalogue people browse, with the technical side one tap away, and it holds three kinds of entry. Design canvas "Library as a catalogue" (claude.ai/artifact/Mf6XKKQQ3ijnKCoWB1PMHx): the Library home, a category page and a product page.

Owner: "library default view should be like a cataluge - ecomm experience - all others are technicals"; "can we store templates too in lib?"

Three kinds of entry (owner: ok; names "yes"):
1. Object: one thing (Sofa, RCC slab, casement window).
2. Layout: several Objects arranged together, Objects only, no lines (owner: "only objects"): a toilet's fittings, a meeting room for 8, a kitchen run. Placed with a choice of Keep (one unit linked to its Library entry, updated by a new version) or Break (separate Objects edited freely) (owner: "Break and Keep options").
3. Template: a project's starting rules (Villa, the five workplaces): uses, default Objects, floor heights. templates/ moves into the Library; ids stay (owner: ok).
All three are versioned and never changed in place, pin exact Object versions, have catalogue pages, reach the Library by the same proposal route ("From your projects"), and a firm's own sits in its layer.
A collection (a shopping list, not arranged) fills a project's shortlist; a layout places things on the canvas.

Organization:
- Identity never changes: flat folders, one per entry, so reorganizing never moves files.
- What the kernel needs: kind and what it attaches to (R113).
- How people find it: a browse tree and tags, as data. Tree by what it is for (Seating, Tables and desks, Beds, Storage, Kitchen, Bathroom, Doors and windows, Walls, Floors and slabs, Structure, Lighting and decor, Outdoor); placing filters by what is selected (a wall shows doors and windows).
- Tags: uses (rooms it suits), typology, source (BuilderPluss, a firm, a manufacturer), state (draft, checked, has render version), IFC class.
- Materials (concrete, glass, wood) apart from an Object's parts (frame, seat), each part with its usual material.

Catalogue:
- Home: search with suggestions, category tiles with pictures, collections, rows (new, your firm's own, most used).
- Category: cards (picture, name, one line, key size, swatches, badges), filters, sort.
- Product: gallery (render, 3D, plan symbol), choices (size presets, swatches, sliders within limits: parameters on the Placement), Place on canvas and Add to project, goes with, used in, and a Specifications tab with all that is technical.
- Shortlist per project, the basket (today's pin), first in the canvas's placing panel.
- A picture for every entry, made by itself from its 3D shape; a render where a detailed version has one.

Settled (owner: "go as you suggest"): misplaced ids are left as they are, the browse tree places them; tags kept: uses, typology, source, state, IFC class; materials apart from an Object's parts; a firm's Library is a layer over BuilderPluss's, marked by source; price, supplier and lead time later, with quantities and BOQ; collections and goes-with curated by the owner or the firm, with suggestions learnt from projects; an Object's sizes shown as one product with choices. Builds after R113.
