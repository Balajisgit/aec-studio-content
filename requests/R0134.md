# R134 · Project notes kept whoever works on a project, and carried in model.json

Notes were written only when an assistant chose to (Innowa does; Claude through the connector did not on DLF), and they live in a table on the PC, so an exported model.json had none. The app keeps a note by itself from the log, whoever made the steps (Innowa, Claude through the connector, a run, the owner on the canvas): who did what. The assistant adds the decisions and what is open, and the connector tells Claude to do so at the end of its work, as Innowa does. Notes are written into model.json on export and read back on import; they stay outside the model itself, so undo never takes one. Owner: 'make notes recorded in the model json whoever creates', then 'ok' to keeping them out of the model and in the file.
