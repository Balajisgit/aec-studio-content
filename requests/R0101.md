# R101 · A project's programme: what the brief asks for, against what is drawn

Owner, 15 Nov 2026: 'if we want complete a villa model from concept exploration to fully built 3d model, what is missing'. On the order (programme; brief to programme; concept layouts; between floors and the stair; flat roof; build-up and vertical Objects; quantities, then drawings): 'Ok'. 'Is Programme a kernel thing? Sure?' — no: the programme is what the client wants, not what the model is, so it lives with the project (its own record, outside the model and its undo), one brief for many designs; the kernel gives a tally of what is drawn (rooms by use, counts, areas), and Projects compares the two and shows the gaps as 'Against the brief', apart from the kernel's notes. Owner: 'Ok'; steps 1 and 2 together: 'Ok'. This request: the programme record, the tally, the comparison on the project page and the canvas and for an agent, and a pipeline that writes the programme from a brief. Also decided for later: flat roof first ('Flat roof'); the built model 'accurate enough for quantities and construction drawings'; export 'Yet to decide'; the layout solver 'Start from first principle'.
