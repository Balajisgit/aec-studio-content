# R151 · Every project has a programme, saying where it came from: a brief, a prompt, or drawn (following the drawing quietly until the owner confirms it)

Owner: "any project should record a program even when the brief or starting prompt doesn not include one. even when starts manual", then "agree" and "quietly". A programme always exists, with its source: brief (written from a brief, deck or notes, confirmed by the owner), prompt (written by Innowa from a short ask and the typology, what the prompt leaves open marked assumed), or drawn (written by the Studio from what is drawn: rooms by use, counts, areas, floors). A drawn programme follows the drawing, with no question asked, until the owner confirms it: then it is the target and the checks compare the drawing with it. The typology can say what its kind of building usually has (a kitchen, a stair above one floor, toilets per floor), so even a drawn programme can say what is missing. Programme options (R127) and measures (R129) work with every source.
