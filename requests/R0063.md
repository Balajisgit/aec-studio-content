## The model: primers and their meanings

- **Primers** are the only geometry: a **node** (alone), a **segment** (two nodes; straight or arc), a **face** (a loop of segments, so its nodes too). Until Objects come, the kernel draws only these, coloured and labelled by what they mean.
- **Meanings come in categories**, at most one from each on a primer:
  - **Function** — what it is (wall, fence, door, window, gate, room, plot, parking bay, building footprint, column position…). One per primer. From the **Library**.
  - **Position** — where it sits relative to the face it belongs to (front, rear, side, corner; own setback). From content (`semantics/`).
  - **Status** (existing, proposed, to demolish) — later.
- **Meaning flows to members.** A face's meaning gives its segments and nodes a meaning in its context, worked out automatically and shown greyed: a Plot's segments are plot edges and its nodes plot corners; mark one edge Front and the rear and sides follow. A member meaning names the face it comes from (a segment can be the plot's front and the footprint's north side). Any one can be set by hand (own setback).
- **No parts of a segment.** A door, window or gate is its own segment between two nodes on the wall's line.

## The Library holds the functions

A new, light kind of Library entry: **meaning only** — its name, the primer it goes on, what it is for; the Library check takes it without a 2D symbol or 3D. Wall, Door, Window, Fence, Gate, Room, Plot, Parking bay, Building footprint, Column position (and furniture and fixtures, by the entries already there). Later the same entry gets its Object (thickness, layers, 3D, parameters), and attaching it brings the real geometry. A firm adds its own functions in its Library layer.

## What goes from the kernel and the canvas

- Wall, door and window as kernel kinds; rooms bounded by walls; slabs; Placements; wall thickness, corners, openings cut, the 3D wall builder; the 2.x view. (Back later, from Objects.)
- Canvas tools: **Select, Line, Face, Point**. The inspector shows a row per category: Function (from the Library) and Position (from content), and the member meanings greyed.

## Existing projects are converted (one logged step, where undo stops)

Walls → segments with function Wall; doors and windows → their own segments (the wall split at their ends) with function Door or Window; rooms → faces with function Room; slabs → their faces with function Slab; Placements → nodes with the function of their Library entry (its Object kept, to attach later); a plot as now.
