# R129 · Measures per typology: what options are compared on, declared in content by the firm and by the project

Each typology template lists its measures (name, how it is worked out from what the app counts, which way is better); a firm changes them in its own template or overrides, and a project can change them too (owner: 'can be declared in content as per the firm and also as per the project'). Every typology also shows the core set: programme met %, rooms under size, net and gross area, efficiency (net/gross). Workplace: seats, people per seat, m2 per seat, meeting seats per 10 people, collaboration and focus seats. Villa: bedrooms, carpet area, built-up area, efficiency, then FAR and ground coverage once a plot is drawn (saleable area if a firm adds it). Mixed use (R102): measures per section. A measure that needs what is not drawn shows a dash. Core set and villa set decided by Claude at the owner's word ('so u decide this').
