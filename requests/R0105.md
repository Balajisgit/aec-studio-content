# R105 · What a room holds (seats, beds, covers): the kernel counts, the typology and Projects judge; the room-use rules move out of the kernel

Owner, 15 Nov 2026: 'Does seat should touch kernel? Is not a specific requirement?' — right: a seat is a workplace word. What is general is how many of something a room holds (seats, covers, pupils, beds, bays). Revised split, owner: 'ok': the kernel only counts (each room's area, and the Objects standing in it); the template says what a use counts and how much room one takes, which Objects provide how many, and the typology's standing ratios; Projects works out the capacity (furniture placed wins, else an estimate from area), lets the programme ask in people, and says the gaps. On the room-use rules added with Villa (a room with no use, under its use's size, with no window, uses kept apart), which the kernel checked: 'Move to projects'. So those notes become Projects' (typology notes), shown with the kernel's under To look at and given to an agent with project_look; the kernel's own notes say nothing about use, except that a use open to the sky needs no door. Briefs come as headcount and as lists of rooms ('Both'); density figures: 'Common'.
