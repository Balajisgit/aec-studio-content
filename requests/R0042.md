# R42 · Windows stacked one above the other on a wall through two floors are refused as overlapping

Found while drawing test4, not said by the owner. On a segment with top on a higher level, the kernel's overlap check (I6) looks only at position along the wall and ignores sill and height, so a ground floor window (sill 900) and a first floor window (sill 3900) at the same station are refused. The windows had to be staggered sideways. The check should also compare the vertical range.
