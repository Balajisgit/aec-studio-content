# R102 · Mixed use: several typologies in one project, and a programme in sections

Owner, 15 Nov 2026: 'What if a project is a mixed use?' and 'how the programmed will we written? In case of mixed use?'; then 'Ok let's see that later'. Discussed, not decided: a part of a building is an outline (the unit agreed for apartments) that says which typology applies inside it, so rooms there take that typology's uses and sizes, and the shared core follows the building's own; the construction kit (walls, floor height) chosen per part or per level; use names kept from clashing between typologies. The programme in three levels: the whole building (floors, built area, what is shared), parts (each with its typology and what it must hold), and unit kinds where a part repeats (a 2 BHK flat described once, wanted eight times), checked at each level (whole, part, inside a unit). Today's one-list programme stays valid as a programme with one part. To be designed with units and apartments. Open questions put to the owner: which mixes he designs; split by floor or side by side; whether briefs give counts or area mixes.
