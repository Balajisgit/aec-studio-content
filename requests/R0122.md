# R122 · When an app step fails, Innowa does it by hand inside the run

A playbook's app step can name a fallback: who does it by hand and what they must make. When the app step's checks fail, the run hands the step to Innowa (or Claude through the connector) instead of stopping; the same checks and the owner's decisions follow.
