# R64 · Attach Objects to meanings: Library Objects give primers their real geometry and parameters (walls, doors, windows on segments; slabs and rooms on faces; furniture and columns on nodes)

The owner (11 Nov 2026), after R63 merged: "do this" — the step R63 left for later: "next will attach objects -(the real geometry with parameters)". A role with a function gets a Library Object (objectRef); the Object brings the geometry and parameters: along a segment (a wall's thickness, layers and height; a door or window sized to its segment), over a face (a slab's depth, a room's finishes) or at a node (furniture, columns, as now). Plan and 3D come from the Objects; a primer without an Object stays drawn by its meaning.
