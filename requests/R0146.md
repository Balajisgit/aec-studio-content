# R146 · Level names and room numbering as a scheme in the template, per firm

Owner: 'the floor system and numbering may differ from firm to firm', then 'ok'. Today level names are hard-coded (the canvas names a new level First floor, then Level 3…; the programme's floor names Ground floor, First floor… are fixed in the Studio's code) and room numbers are typed by hand. The template (and a firm's own, and a project's overrides) carries a scheme: how levels are named going up and down (Ground/First, Level 0/1, L01; basements B1 or Lower ground; mezzanines, podium) and how rooms are numbered (level and sequence like 1.01, by zone or wing). The canvas, the programme's floors and Innowa follow it; nothing in the code names a level. Floor systems (beams and slab, flat slab, PT, steel deck, load-bearing) come the same way, through R144's relationship rules in a firm's template.
