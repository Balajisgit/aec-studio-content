# R156 · Walls and what meets them: justify (centre, left, right), align to a face, corner style and priority as Object parameters

Owner on wall joins: "Rather than replacing, give as configurations like centre , left and right justifications", then "Object params is ok as u suggest" and "When two different objects meets(either top or side), face orientation is the params as like justification for similar objects", then "Ok build". Today stays the default (centre, mitre, thicker carries on). justify: where a wall, fence, railing or parapet sits on its line (centre, left, right of the line as drawn). align: which face of the other Object one lines up with when two different Objects meet, on top or at the side (beam on a wall, door or window in a wall depth, parapet over the wall below). corner (mitre or butt) and priority (who carries on), read from both walls. All are Object parameters: the Object default, the template Construction, or one wall by hand; never a canvas setting. The kernel solves, every canvas draws its answer; anything placed on a wall follows the wall body. Objects that only meet (no host) get align from the Studio relationships. Canvas: the Wall tool draws as centre, left or right; the inspector flips; snapping to faces. R120 (net area beside a step) is folded in.
