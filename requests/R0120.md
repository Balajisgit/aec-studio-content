# R120 · Net room area slightly off where walls of different thickness meet in a straight line

Noted by Innowa, not asked by the owner. Where two lines of a room's side are in a straight line but carry different wall thicknesses (or one has no wall), the app's net area seems to slant the inner face along one of them instead of making a square step. L-type office floor: Open office net 247.13 in the app, 247.10 by hand (the drywall 100 S29, 2000 long, in line with the glazed 50 partition S37: 25 x 2000 / 2 = 0.025 m²). test1: Corridor 37.12 in the app, 37.17 by the script (S15, 1000 long, no wall, in line with S14, 200 thick: 100 x 1000 / 2 = 0.05 m²); Reception & Waiting 37.98 against 38.02. The cause is inferred from these differences; the app's code was not read.
