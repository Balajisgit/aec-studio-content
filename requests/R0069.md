# R69 · Easy drawing 2/5: typed lengths, live dimensions, edit a dimension

The owner (11 Nov 2026): "ya make it as requests to go by the order". Second of five. While drawing, type a length (3600, Enter) to place the next point that far in the pointer's direction; angle snaps at 15° and 45° besides Shift for square; lengths shown along the lines as they are drawn; click a line's dimension to change it (its far point moves); snap to wall faces as well as centre lines and the grid.
