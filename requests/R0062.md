## The model, version 3.0: three layers

1. **Geometry (the base sketch):** nodes, segments (straight or arc) and faces on a level. Faces are stored with their own ids, so a room keeps its name and role when a wall moves; a split face keeps its role on both parts ("Kitchen", "Kitchen 2"), merged faces keep the larger one's. Geometry means nothing by itself.
2. **Semantics:** an assignment gives a node, a segment, a face, part of a segment (from a station to a station) or one edge of a face a **role**: wall, room, door, window, plot, front edge, WC… Roles are listed in content (`semantics/`), the platform's and each firm's in its knowledge layer (a firm's role of the same name wins inside that firm).
3. **Parameters:** each assignment may attach an **Object** with its parameters (the Library's parametric Objects, extended to segments, parts of segments and faces): a wall Object (brick 230, height 3000) on a segment, a door Object on part of a wall, setbacks on the plot. A role declares the parameters it takes, with limits; the panel is built from that.

**Behaviours** stay in the kernel, few and general: wall-like (joins at corners, thickness, height), opening (cuts its wall), room-like (bounded, areas), area (a face with no walls: a plot, a parking bay), placed object, none. A role names its behaviour; a firm's role uses existing behaviours only. Everything drawn (wall outlines, slabs, 3D) is derived from geometry + roles + Objects and never stored.

Existing projects move to 3.0 on first open, with a snapshot to roll back: walls → segment + wall role + a wall Object from their thickness and height; openings → door or window on part of the segment; spaces → faces with the room role; Placements → nodes with their Object; model.site → a plot face. Tried on a copy of production, then staging.

## Solvers: pipeline content, not app code

- A solver is a file in the platform's content (`solvers/*.js`), changed only by pull request: a pure function from the roles it asks for, their parameters and the run's settings to **candidates** (each with geometry to preview, its numbers, and the model operations it would make). It never writes the model.
- It runs on the server in a locked worker: no files, no network, a time and memory limit. Firms ask for new solvers through Request to BuilderPluss; their pipelines can use any platform solver.
- The app gives solvers a small geometry library (point in polygon, distance to an edge, offset, area, rotate).
- One general decision form, **pick a candidate**, replaces pick-footprint: the candidates as a list and as outlines on the plan; the pick applies its operations as one undoable step, then the checks run.

## Pipelines on the canvas

A **Run** tab in the canvas's right panel: start a pipeline (its inputs as a form); follow its steps; at a decision, the choices in the panel and on the plan (candidates as outlines, hover to preview, click to choose), the decision's button applies it; cancel and retry. The project page, the chat and the canvas show the same run, and an answer in one updates the others.
