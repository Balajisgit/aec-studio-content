# R157 · Units, scale and calibration: lengths in mm, m or feet and inches; a drawing scale for pictures and prints; a plan placed under the drawing and calibrated by two points

Owner: "Also calibration, scale and uom settings are needed.", then "Yes. Build it" to this reading. Units: show and type lengths in mm, m, or feet and inches (areas in m² or sq ft); the model stays in whole mm inside. Scale: the drawing scale (1:50, 1:100) for plan pictures and PDFs, with text and symbol sizes. Calibration: a scanned plan, PDF or image placed under the drawing to trace; pick two points and type the real distance. Order: units, then scale, then calibration.
