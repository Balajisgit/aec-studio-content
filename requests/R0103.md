# R103 · Model defaults for the modern workplace: five verticals

Owner, 15 Nov 2026: 'Now create model defaults for modern workspce for different verticas'. The verticals were not named, so Claude chose five (the owner's earlier word on such lists: 'U go as per ur decision'): technology and global capability centres; corporate and professional or financial services; co-working and flex; contact centre and business-process; creative studio and media. Each is a template: its uses with the firm's smallest sizes (Claude's numbers, the owner's to change), the uses kept apart, and an office construction kit (glazed facade, drywall and glazed partitions, carpet, vinyl or polished concrete on an RCC slab, 4.2 to 4.5 m floors), with the seven Library Objects that kit needs. In the app: a room against a glazed outside wall has its daylight (it is not asked for a window), and a note's examples name the model's own uses. Not in this request: sizing by the seat (density, seats in the programme and the tally), units and tenancies, furniture for offices.
