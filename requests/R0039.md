# R39 · Kernel: one schema for 2D and 3D (heights through levels, spaces as volumes, kernel solids)

One model for plan and 3D; the kernel works out both views and every canvas only draws them through its adapter.

Part 1 (this request):
- Segments can rise through levels: optional top {level, offset} instead of a height; the kernel works out each segment's bottom and top (segmentZ).
- Spaces are volumes: optional ceiling {level, offset} (a ceiling on a higher level makes a double-height space, e.g. an atrium) and open: true for terraces and courtyards (area only). Volume derived (spaceExtent).
- Voids: where a lower space rises through a level, that level's slab gets a hole (levelVoids, slab holes).
- Derived views in the kernel: solidsOf (walls cut round openings, slabs with holes, space volumes, leaves, panes, Placements, each with its source id) and planOf (a level's plan, including segments passing up through it from below). The built-in canvas's mesh.js uses solidsOf.
- Hosting rules settled: columns are Placements at a node (role column), beams are segments of kind beam that never bound rooms; a check notes a beam end with no column or wall at its node.

Part 2 (later): walls passing through a level bound that level's rooms (nodes joined by position), canvases and the Canvas13 adapter draw planOf's passing walls, explicit slab elements on faces, IFC export of volumes.
