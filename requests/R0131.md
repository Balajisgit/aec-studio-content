# R131 · Test fit: deep floors with a central core leave out most rooms and seat too few

On the DLF 9A plate (62 x 75 m, central core, two service cores, 9 m grid) workplace-test-fit 3.0.0 set rooms only in strips along two outside walls: 53 of 92 rooms left out and 12.9 m2 a seat. With the rooms drawn first it seated 224 of 360 (desks kept a module off every room; the room ratios added a second cafeteria). The layout was drawn by hand instead: rooms round the cores, six- and four-seat benches mixed to use the 7.8 m clear between columns (355 seats). The test fit should set rooms round cores as well as along the facade, and fill bays with mixed benches.
