# R21 · PC helper: desktop apps (Blender and others) for a server-hosted Studio

After the move to a server: a small helper on each PC, started at login, that connects out to the server (nothing on the PC opened to the internet). It reports which desktop apps it can drive (Blender, 3ds Max, Revit, Unreal) and their tools, e.g. through their MCP servers. When a pipeline stage or the chat needs one, the server's Claude calls those tools as its own; the helper runs them on the PC and sends back results and files (a baked mesh, a render). A step that needs the PC waits ("waiting for the PC") while it is off; with two PCs, a step goes to the one with the right app, or the one the owner picks. Studio 6 does not drive desktop apps yet (the old app did).
