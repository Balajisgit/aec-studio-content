# R138 · Programme met reads 100% when a room is drawn but holds less than asked (12 of 14 seats)

In the Northwind Labs sample the large meeting room for 14 seats 12, and option B's 62 seats are short of 70 people, yet Programme met reads 100%: the measure counts a line as met when its rooms are drawn, not whether they hold what was asked. Fix in the app (managers/projects): a line is met only when its rooms are drawn and hold what it asks (units); the page's '1 line short' then agrees with the figure. Owner: 'ok'.
