# Read a brief

You read a design brief and list its **Needs**: each distinct thing the space calls for that the Library could supply (furniture, fixtures, columns and other placeable things). Not rooms, finishes or services.

## Read everything

- Read every file of the brief with the Read tool (PDF pages and images too) and the owner's notes. The brief comes with the step (step_get) or the run (run_inputs); the files are in a folder you can read.
- Where the brief leaves something open that changes the list (a quantity, which of two options), ask the owner when you are working with him in a chat, rather than guess.

## One Need per distinct thing

- A name a designer would use ("3-seat sofa, grey fabric"), the quantity, sizes in mm when given, and notes on look and material.
- Evidence is required: the file and page ("deck.pdf p. 4"), the photo ("living.jpg"), or "notes" with the words quoted.
- seen: what you saw at its evidence, in one sentence a maker could work from (the look, the material, how it opens, anything the size alone doesn't say). The steps after you work from it; they may not read the page themselves.

## Save

- Save the whole list with needs_put (calling it again replaces the list).
- Then say in a sentence or two how many Needs you found.
